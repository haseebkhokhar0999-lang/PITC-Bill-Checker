1. What is a Duplicate Electricity Bill & When Do You Need It?

Understanding Duplicate Electricity Bills for All 10 DISCOs in Pakistan

A duplicate electricity bill is an authentic computerized copy of your monthly electricity bill statement issued by your power distribution company (DISCO) and retrieved directly from the central Power Information Technology Company (PITC) billing database. Under NEPRA regulations, a duplicate bill printed online carries the exact same legal validity as the paper bill delivered to your doorstep.

Common Situations When You Need a Duplicate Bill:

  • 1
    Paper Bill Lost or Misplaced: If your monthly paper bill was misplaced, wet, torn, or lost before payment could be made.
  • 2
    Delayed or Non-Delivery: When the meter reader does not deliver your paper bill on time, downloading a duplicate bill prevents missing the due date and avoids late payment surcharges.
  • 3
    Bank Over-the-Counter Payment: Commercial banks and post offices require a printed bill with clear, scannable barcodes and payment counterfoils.
  • 4
    Valid Proof of Residence & Records: A duplicate bill serves as valid proof of address for opening bank accounts, passport renewal, new utility connections, and tax filings.
💡 Validity Note: Any duplicate electricity bill downloaded or printed from the central PITC portal contains authentic barcodes, meter reading photos, and standard 3-tier bank counterfoils (Bank Copy, DISCO Copy, Consumer Copy) accepted at all commercial banks across Pakistan.

3. How to Check & Open Original Bill on PITC Portal

Direct Access to PITC Central Billing Server for Real-Time Bill Statements

Power Information Technology Company (PITC at bill.pitc.com.pk) is the central computerized data repository operating under the Ministry of Energy (Power Division) in Pakistan. PITC manages the billing calculation engines, customer ledgers, and duplicate web bill portals for all 10 DISCOs across the country.

Step-by-Step Instructions to Open Bill on PITC:

  • 1
    Enter 14-Digit Reference Number: Type your exact 14 continuous digits into the input box above. Do not type any spaces or dashes.
  • 2
    Choose Urban or Rural: Select Urban ('U') or Rural ('R') based on the classification printed on your bill.
  • 3
    Click "Check Duplicate Bill": When submitted, your browser opens https://bill.pitc.com.pk/gbill.aspx?refno=<your-14-digit-number> in a new tab.
  • 4
    Review Your Live Statement: The computerized bill loads in full high-resolution detail showing your name, address, current meter reading, billing month, units consumed, issue date, due date, and payable amount.
⚡ Direct & Safe Connection: Our portal uses the direct-PITC method without intermediate server storage, ensuring 100% data authenticity straight from the central power billing servers.

4. Step-by-Step Guide to Download & Print Duplicate Bill

Save as PDF on Mobile/Computer or Print Authentic Hard Copy with Barcodes

Once your computerized electricity bill opens in your browser through the PITC portal, you can easily save it as a PDF document on your device or print a paper hard copy for bank deposit.

Detailed Printing & PDF Download Steps:

  • 1
    Open Print Command: On a computer keyboard, press Ctrl + P (Windows) or Cmd + P (Mac). On mobile Chrome or Safari, tap the three dots or share icon and tap "Print".
  • 2
    Select Destination (PDF or Paper):
    • To save digitally: Change Destination to "Save as PDF".
    • To print paper copy: Select your connected office or home printer.
  • 3
    Adjust Print Settings:
    • Layout: Portrait
    • Paper Size: A4
    • Margins: Default or Minimum
    • Options: Check the box for "Background graphics" so company logos, table lines, and barcodes render sharply.
  • 4
    Save or Print: Click Save to store the duplicate bill PDF in your Downloads folder, or click Print to generate the physical paper slip.
🖨️ Bank Acceptance Tip: Make sure the bottom counterfoils and barcode lines are printed clearly without truncation so bank tellers or barcode scanners at 1Link payment kiosks can read your consumer reference number immediately.

5. How to Check Bill Payment Status (Paid / Unpaid) Online

Real-Time Banking Settlement Verification via PITC CCMS Database

After paying your electricity bill through an online banking app, mobile wallet, or physical bank counter, it is good practice to verify that your payment was successfully reconciled in central DISCO records.

Understanding Payment Badges:

  • ✓
    PAID (ادا شدہ): The green badge confirms that your payment cleared through the 1Link or commercial bank network and has been posted to your consumer account. No overdue charges or arrears exist for the current cycle.
  • ⚠️
    UNPAID (غیر ادا شدہ): Indicates that payment has not yet been processed or cleared into central billing servers. Settle your balance before the due date to prevent disconnection or late surcharges.

Convenient Digital Payment Channels:

You can pay electricity bills online without standing in bank queues using:
• Mobile Wallets: JazzCash, Easypaisa, SadaPay, NayaPay
• Internet Banking & Mobile Apps: HBL, UBL, MCB, Meezan Bank, Bank Alfalah, ABL, NBP, Bank of Punjab
• 1Link ATM Kiosks: Available at over 16,000 ATMs across Pakistan

⏱️ Reconciliation Window: Online digital payments via mobile apps usually take between 24 to 48 banking hours to reflect on the PITC CCMS server. Always save your transaction ID and confirmation SMS as proof of timely payment.

    PAID (ادا شدہ): سبز رنگ کا بیج یہ ظاہر کرتا ہے کہ بینک یا ون لنک کے ذریعے آپ کا بل کلیئر ہو چکا ہے اور کمپنی کے ریکارڈ میں رواں ماہ کے کوئی بقایا جات موجود نہیں ہیں۔

6. Bill Due Date, Late Payment Surcharge & Installment Rules

Payment Deadlines, NEPRA Late Payment Penalties, and Bill Installment Procedures

Every computerized electricity bill issued in Pakistan lists two separate payable figures to incentivize timely payment before the utility billing cycle closes:

Two Payable Amounts on Your Bill:

  • 1
    Payable Within Due Date: The net billed electricity amount covering energy units consumed, fuel cost adjustment (FCA), financing surcharges, electricity duty, and GST active for that month.
  • 2
    Payable After Due Date: Includes the Late Payment Surcharge (LPS). Under NEPRA regulations, a late payment fee of approximately 8% to 10% is added if payment is deposited past the due date deadline.

How to Apply for Bill Installments (اقساط):

If your seasonal bill is unusually high and you cannot pay the full lump sum at once, consumers have the legal right under the Consumer Service Manual to apply for installments:
• Visit Your SDO Office: Go to your nearest Sub-Divisional Office (SDO) or Customer Services Center before the due date expires.
• Submit Request: Present your duplicate bill and CNIC copy to the authorized officer (SDO or Revenue Officer).
• Approval Limits: SDOs can generally approve 2 to 3 monthly installments for domestic and commercial connections.
• Get Computerized Vouchers: Once approved, computerized installment slips are generated, allowing you to pay the first installment immediately at any bank.

⚠️ Important Rule: Installments must be requested before the original due date expires. Once the due date passes, late payment surcharge is automatically added to the arrears ledger.

7. How to Check Duplicate Bill by 10-Digit Customer ID

Alternative Lookup Method for Consumers Without a 14-Digit Reference Number

In addition to the traditional 14-digit reference number, modern computerized utility connections in Pakistan are assigned a unique 10-digit Customer ID. Both numbers point to the identical billing ledger on the PITC server.

Difference Between Customer ID and Reference Number:

  • 1
    14-Digit Reference Number: Encodes your sub-division code, feeder block, and meter sequence. It is the primary identifier used for quick bill checks and bank payments.
  • 2
    10-Digit Customer ID: A computerized consumer account identifier printed near your name on the bill statement. Useful when your reference number digits are worn out or illegible on an old bill slip.

How to Look Up Bills with Customer ID on PITC:

1. Visit the central PITC portal at bill.pitc.com.pk.
2. Select the "Customer ID" tab instead of Reference Number.
3. Enter your complete 10-digit Customer ID into the search input.
4. Click submit to view and print your duplicate electricity bill statement.

💡 Search Tip: The fast search form at the top of this page is optimized for the standard 14-digit Reference Number used on 99% of electricity bills across Pakistan.

9. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) About Duplicate Electricity Bills

Common Questions Regarding Duplicate Bill Download, Validity & Payment

Q1: Is an online duplicate bill accepted at all bank branches in Pakistan?

Yes. Any duplicate electricity bill downloaded or printed from the PITC portal carries authentic machine-readable barcodes, customer account information, and standardized bank payment counterfoils. Commercial banks, post offices, and Omni/JazzCash agents accept it without question.

Q2: Is there any fee or charge for downloading a duplicate electricity bill online?

No. Checking, viewing, downloading, and printing duplicate bills through our portal and the central PITC portal is 100% free of cost.

Q3: Why does the PITC portal show "Bill Not Found" when I enter my reference number?

This occurs if: (a) You typed fewer or more than 14 digits, (b) You included dashes or spaces, (c) The Urban/Rural selection (U/R) did not match your connection, or (d) The new connection's computerized billing record has not yet been uploaded for the current month.

Q4: Can I view previous months' billing records on a duplicate bill?

Yes. Every duplicate bill includes a 12-month billing and consumption history table showing units consumed, billed amounts, and payment records for each preceding month.

Q5: What should I do if my payment was deducted from bank app but duplicate bill still shows UNPAID?

Bank clearance through 1Link can take 24 to 48 banking hours to reflect on the PITC CCMS database. Always keep your digital payment transaction ID or SMS as proof. If status does not update after 48 hours, present your receipt at your local sub-division office.
