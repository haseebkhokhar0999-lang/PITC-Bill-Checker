2. Nature of Service & Disclaimer

Bijli Bill Checker acts solely as a convenient interface that retrieves public electricity billing information provided by PITC and Pakistani electricity distribution companies (DISCOs), including:

  • LESCO (Lahore Electric Supply Company)
  • MEPCO (Multan Electric Power Company)
  • IESCO (Islamabad Electric Supply Company)
  • FESCO (Faisalabad Electric Supply Company)
  • GEPCO (Gujranwala Electric Power Company)
  • PESCO (Peshawar Electric Supply Company)

We do not generate, modify, or recalculate electricity tariffs, fuel price adjustments (FPA), taxes, or bill amounts. All billing records reflect billing data provided by PITC and the respective DISCO.

3. Bill Payment Status & Verification

Payment indicators (such as "PAID" or "UNPAID") displayed on our portal reflect status markers and bank settlement feeds provided by PITC and distribution companies. While online payments (via 1Link, JazzCash, Easypaisa, or commercial bank branches) typically update within 24 to 48 banking hours, consumers are advised to keep their payment transaction receipts or stamped bank counter slips as permanent proof of payment.

4. Duplicate Bill & Original Copy Printing

The original bill printing feature reproduces the PITC duplicate bill format intended for consumer records, tax deduction verification, or payment at designated bank counters and digital utility payment kiosks. Users are permitted to print or save personal duplicate bill copies without restriction.
