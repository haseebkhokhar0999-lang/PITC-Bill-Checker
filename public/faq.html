Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about checking, verifying, paying, and printing electricity bills in Pakistan.

⚡ Checking & Finding Your Bill
  1. Select your city from the top right dropdown or choose your electricity company (LESCO, MEPCO, IESCO, FESCO, GEPCO, PESCO, HESCO, QESCO, or SEPCO).
  2. Enter your 14-digit reference number found on your physical electricity bill slip.
  3. Click the blue "Check Bill →" button. Your complete bill voucher will load immediately with consumer name, payable amount, due date, payment status, and consumption history.

The reference number is printed in the top header box of your electricity bill, usually labeled REFERENCE NO (حوالہ نمبر) right above your consumer name and address. It is a 14-digit sequence (e.g., 06 11353 0462901 U). You only need to type the digits into our search box.

💳 Payment Status & Bank Verification

When you check your bill on Bijli Bill Checker:

  • PAID ✓ (ادا شدہ): If the bill payment has cleared in the PITC bank clearing system, you will see a prominent green PAID badge and a verified stamp.
  • UNPAID ⚠️ (غیر ادا شدہ): If the bill is awaiting payment, the status displays UNPAID with the payable amount and due date.

When you pay via mobile apps (JazzCash, Easypaisa, Nayapay, Bank Alfalah, HBL, etc.) or at a bank branch, the transaction passes through the 1Link/NIFT settlement system to PITC databases. It normally takes 24 to 48 banking hours for the PITC portal to reflect the "PAID" status. As long as you have your digital transaction ID (TID) or stamped receipt, your payment is secure.

You can pay your electricity bill instantly from your smartphone using:

  • JazzCash & Easypaisa Apps: Go to Bill Payment → Electricity → Select your DISCO → Enter 14-digit reference number.
  • Bank Mobile Banking & ATMs (1Link): Any Pakistani banking app (HBL, Meezan, MCB, UBL, Alfalah, Allied, NBP) under Utility Bill Payment.
  • Post Offices & Commercial Bank Counters: Take a printed copy of your original bill voucher.
🖨️ Printing & Duplicate Bill Copy

After your bill details load, look at the action buttons toolbar:

  1. Click the blue "🖨️ Print Original Bill (اصل بل کا پرنٹ)" button.
  2. This immediately generates the 100% authentic PITC duplicate bill document with company emblems, barcodes, meter reading tables, Urdu guidelines, and bank cut slips.
  3. The standard browser print dialog opens automatically formatted for standard A4 paper. You can also save it as a PDF!

Yes, absolutely. The duplicate bill generated by our system is retrieved directly from PITC (Power Information Technology Company) portals with genuine verification barcodes. All commercial banks, NADRA e-Sahulat franchises, Easypaisa shops, and Pakistan Post offices accept this duplicate copy for payment and record keeping.

❓ Common Errors & Troubleshooting

Our intelligent system automatically searches all 9 Pakistani DISCOs in parallel if your reference number does not belong to the currently selected company. If it is still not found, double check that you have typed all 14 digits accurately without typing consumer ID or CNIC numbers by mistake.
