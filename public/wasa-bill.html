Guide 1 WASA Water Information

1. WASA Lahore Water Bill Overview & Key Differences from Electricity Bills

Understanding Municipal Water Supply & Drainage Across Lahore

Water and Sanitation Agency (WASA) Lahore is the public municipal utility operating under the Housing, Urban Development and Public Health Engineering Department (HUD & PHED), Government of the Punjab. WASA manages clean tap water supply, tube-wells, sewerage networks, and stormwater drainage for over 10 million citizens in Lahore.

Key Differences: WASA Water Bills vs Electricity DISCO Bills:

  • 1
    Consumer Identification: WASA Lahore bills use an 8-digit Account Number printed at the top of the bill, whereas electricity DISCOs use a 14-digit reference number.
  • 2
    Separate Billing System: PITC (bill.pitc.com.pk) is strictly for power distribution companies (LESCO, MEPCO, etc.). WASA water bills are verified and generated via WASA Lahore's own portal at wasa.punjab.gov.pk.
  • 3
    Billing Slabs & Criteria: Residential water bills are calculated based on property size (e.g. up to 5 Marlas, 7 Marlas, 10 Marlas, 1 Kanal, 2 Kanals) or metered consumption, plus standardized sewerage rates.
💡 Coverage Towns: WASA Lahore provides municipal water services to all administrative towns across Lahore including Gulberg, Samanabad, Ravi Town, Shalimar, Data Ganj Bakhsh, Allama Iqbal Town, Nishtar Town, and Aziz Bhatti Town.

Guide 2 Online Bill Verification

2. How to Check WASA Lahore Water Bill Online (Using 8-Digit Account Number)

Step-by-Step Online Verification via the WASA Lahore Website

Checking your WASA Lahore water bill online is fast and simple. Follow these steps to view your active bill statement:

Step-by-Step Online Bill Inquiry:

  • 1
    Find Your 8-Digit Account Number: Check any old paper water bill. Your 8-digit Account Number (e.g. 12345678) is printed in the consumer information box at the top.
  • 2
    Visit WASA Lahore Website: Open your browser and navigate to the WASA Lahore portal at https://wasa.punjab.gov.pk/.
  • 3
    Go to Duplicate Bill Section: Click on Duplicate Bill or Online Services → Consumer Bill.
  • 4
    Enter 8-Digit Account Number: Type your exact 8-digit Account Number into the input field without any dashes or spaces.
  • 5
    Submit & View Bill: Click Submit. Your complete computerized water and sanitation bill statement will open on screen, showing consumer name, payable amount, and due date.
🔍 Account Number Tip: If your account number has fewer than 8 digits, add leading zeros if prompted, or verify the number directly on your physical bill slip.

Guide 4 Bill Breakdown

4. How to Read Your WASA Water Bill (Charges, Sewerage & Due Date)

Understanding Water Supply Tariffs, Sewerage Rates & Due Dates

A standard WASA Lahore bill details your property classification, consumption rates, and municipal dues:

Key Bill Elements Explained:

  • 1
    Account Number & Consumer Info: The 8-digit Account Number, consumer name, registered address, and property plot size (e.g. 5 Marla, 10 Marla, 1 Kanal).
  • 2
    Water Supply Charges: The fee charged for municipal piped drinking water, calculated according to property area slabs or metered volume.
  • 3
    Sewerage & Drainage Charges: Fixed percentage or municipal rate charged for maintaining stormwater drains, disposal stations, and sewerage channels.
  • 4
    Billing Month & Due Date: Shows the billing cycle month and the last deadline to clear your bill without penalty.
  • 5
    Late Payment Surcharge: Penalty amount added to the bill if payment is submitted after the specified due date.
  • 6
    Arrears (Previous Dues): Any outstanding unpaid amounts from previous billing cycles carried forward to the current invoice.
💡 Due Date Advice: Paying your WASA bill on or before the due date avoids extra surcharges and ensures continuous uninterrupted water supply.

5. How to Pay WASA Lahore Bill Online

Modern Digital Payment Options via e-Pay Punjab, Banking Apps & Wallets

WASA Lahore bill payments can be made conveniently from your smartphone without visiting a bank branch:

Online Payment Channels:

  • 1
    e-Pay Punjab (Mobile App & Web): Open the official e-Pay Punjab app → Select WASA → Enter your 8-digit Account Number → Generate PSID challan → Settle via 1Link, mobile banking, or wallet.
  • 2
    Mobile Wallets (JazzCash, Easypaisa, SadaPay, NayaPay): Open your wallet → Tap Bill Payment / Utilities → Select Water → Choose WASA Lahore → Enter your 8-digit Account Number → Confirm transaction.
  • 3
    Commercial Banking Apps: Log in to your banking application (HBL, Meezan, Bank Alfalah, UBL, MCB, ABL, NBP) → Go to Utility Bill Payments → Select WASA Lahore → Type Account Number → Pay dues.
  • 4
    1Link ATMs: Pay directly using your ATM debit card at any 1Link ATM across Lahore and Pakistan.
  • 5
    Over-the-Counter Cash: Deposit cash along with your printed duplicate bill at any authorized commercial bank or post office.
⚡ Instant Confirmation: Payments made via e-Pay Punjab or mobile banking apps are cleared immediately in the WASA billing database.

Guide 7 Frequently Asked Questions

7. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) About WASA Water Bills

Quick Answers to Common WASA Lahore Billing Questions

  • Q1
    Where can I find my 8-digit WASA Account Number?
    Your Account Number is an 8-digit number printed on the upper left or top section of your monthly paper water bill.
  • Q2
    Can I check my WASA bill on the PITC electricity portal?
    No. PITC (bill.pitc.com.pk) is exclusively for electricity DISCOs. WASA Lahore water bills are verified through the WASA Lahore portal at wasa.punjab.gov.pk.
  • Q3
    Is a printed duplicate WASA bill accepted at commercial banks?
    Yes. A duplicate copy printed from the WASA portal is valid and accepted at all commercial banks, Pakistan Post, and collection agents.
  • Q4
    What is the customer emergency helpline for WASA Lahore?
    The official 24/7 WASA helpline is 1334, and WhatsApp support is available at 0334-1334470.

    نہیں، پی آئی ٹی سی صرف بجلی کمپنیوں کے لیے ہے۔ واسا کا بل wasa.punjab.gov.pk پر چیک کیا جاتا ہے۔
  • س3
    کیا پرنٹ شدہ ڈپلیکیٹ بل بینک میں قبول ہوتا ہے؟
    جی ہاں، واسا پورٹل سے پرنٹ شدہ ڈپلیکیٹ بل تمام کمرشل بینکوں اور ڈاک خانوں میں ادائیگی کے لیے مکمل طور پر قابل قبول ہے۔
  • س4
