4. SEPCO Bill Due Date, Surcharge and Installments Due Date Deadlines, Late Payment Fees, and Official Installment Procedures

Every SEPCO electricity bill specifies two different amounts: Amount Payable Within Due Date and Amount Payable After Due Date (which includes late payment penalties).

Late Payment Surcharge:

If payment is made after the due date, a Late Payment Surcharge is levied in accordance with NEPRA regulations (typically 8% to 10% on domestic connections). Paying your bill on or before the due date saves you from these extra charges.

How to Apply for SEPCO Bill Installments:

If your bill amount is high and you cannot pay it in a single lump sum, SEPCO provides consumers the legal facility to pay in installments:

  • •
    Visit the Relevant SEPCO Office: Go to your nearest SEPCO Customer Facilitation Center or Revenue Office before the due date expires.
  • •
    Submit an Installment Application: Depending on the total bill amount, the authorized officer (SDO or XEN) can grant approval for 2 to 3 monthly installments.
  • •
    Obtain Computerized Vouchers: Once approved, computerized installment vouchers are issued, which can be paid immediately at any commercial bank or online portal.

5. SEPCO Bill Tariff Understanding Consumer Categories, Slabs, and Billing Criteria

SEPCO electricity bills are calculated based on a structured billing tariff approved by NEPRA. Your total bill amount depends on your designated consumer category, with each category subject to specific electricity rates and billing slabs based on usage.

Consumer Categories & Billing Slabs:

  • •
    Protected Consumers: Domestic consumers whose monthly electricity usage remains under 200 units consistently for 6 consecutive billing months qualify for protected category status. These households are billed at lower, subsidized base rates to keep essential energy affordable.
  • •
    Lifeline Consumers: Low-income domestic households consuming up to 50 units per billing month are classified as lifeline consumers. This segment receives substantial government relief and subsidies, along with exemptions from specific fuel price adjustments (FPA).
  • •
    Unprotected Consumers: Consumers whose consumption exceeds 200 units in any single billing cycle are classified as unprotected. Once this threshold is crossed, unprotected status applies for the subsequent 6 months, and higher tiered tariff slabs apply where unit rates increase progressively with greater energy consumption.
📌 Tariff Notice: All electricity tariff slabs and adjustments are regulated under NEPRA guidelines and are periodically revised. Always verify your current consumer category and billing slab printed directly on your latest SEPCO bill.

SEPCO کے بجلی بل نیپرا کے منظور شدہ ٹیرف ڈھانچے کے تحت تیار کیے جاتے ہیں۔ آپ کے بل کی رقم کا تعین اس بات پر ہوتا ہے کہ آپ کس صارف کیٹیگری میں آتے ہیں، کیونکہ مختلف کیٹیگریز اور استعمال کی بنیاد پر مختلف ریٹ لاگو ہوتے ہیں۔

صارفین کی کیٹیگریز اور بلنگ سلیب:

  • •
    پروٹیکٹڈ صارفین (Protected Consumers): ایسے گھریلو صارفین جن کا ماہانہ بجلی کا استعمال مسلسل 6 ماہ تک 200 یونٹ سے کم رہتا ہے، وہ پروٹیکٹڈ کیٹیگری میں شمار ہوتے ہیں۔ ان صارفین پر رعایت یافتہ اور کم نرخوں کے تحت بل لاگو ہوتا ہے۔
  • •
    لائف لائن صارفین (Lifeline Consumers): ماہانہ 50 یونٹ تک استعمال کرنے والے کم آمدن گھریلو صارفین کو لائف لائن کیٹیگری میں رکھا جاتا ہے۔ ان کو حکومت کی طرف سے خصوصی سبسڈی ملتی ہے اور فیول پرائس ایڈجسٹمنٹ (FPA) سے چھوٹ حاصل ہوتی ہے۔
  • •
    نان پروٹیکٹڈ صارفین (Unprotected Consumers): اگر کسی بھی ماہ بجلی کی کھپت 200 یونٹ سے بڑھ جائے تو صارف اگلے 6 ماہ کے لیے ان پروٹیکٹڈ زمرے میں آ جاتا ہے۔ اس کیٹیگری میں زیادہ نرخ لاگو ہوتے ہیں اور جوں جوں یونٹس بڑھتے ہیں، ریٹ میں بھی اضافہ ہوتا چلا جاتا ہے۔

6. SEPCO Peak and Off-Peak Hours Seasonal Schedule, 3-Phase Meter Readings, and Energy Saving Tips

SEPCO designates specific peak hours during the day when electricity demand reaches its peak across the distribution network. During these high-demand hours, the electrical grid experiences maximum load, and electricity consumed during peak times is billed at higher tariff rates compared to normal hours.

Note for 3-Phase Meters: For consumers equipped with Time-of-Use (TOU) 3-phase digital meters, peak and off-peak electricity consumption readings are recorded separately. The meter logs peak units on a dedicated register, and those units are charged at a higher peak-rate tariff on your monthly bill.

Seasonal Peak and Off-Peak Hours Schedule:

Season / Months Peak Hours (Higher Rate) Off-Peak Hours (Normal Rate)
Dec to Feb (December – February) 5:00 PM – 9:00 PM Remaining hours (All other 20 hours)
Mar to May (March – May) 6:00 PM – 10:00 PM Remaining hours (All other 20 hours)
Jun to Aug (June – August) 7:00 PM – 11:00 PM Remaining hours (All other 20 hours)
Sep to Nov (September – November) 6:00 PM – 10:00 PM Remaining hours (All other 20 hours)

Practical Tips to Reduce Your Electricity Bill:

Shift heavy-load electrical appliances like refrigerator cooling cycles, electric irons, washing machines, water motors, and air conditioners (ACs) to off-peak hours whenever possible. Keeping peak-hour consumption minimal helps prevent high unit charges and substantially reduces your overall monthly bill.

📌 Important Timing Note: Peak hours follow the seasonal schedule defined by regulatory authorities and can change whenever a new notification is issued. Always verify current notifications or check the peak timing details printed on your latest SEPCO bill.
