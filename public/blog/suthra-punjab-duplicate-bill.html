1. What is the Suthra Punjab Sanitation Bill? Public Waste Management & Hygiene Service Fee

The Suthra Punjab program is the Government of Punjab's flagship province-wide initiative to ensure standardized urban and rural solid waste management, door-to-door trash collection, and drain desilting across all 36 districts of Punjab. To sustain round-the-clock municipal sanitation machinery, nominal sanitation service charges are billed to properties.

2. How to Check Suthra Punjab Duplicate Bill Online (Step-by-Step) Accessing the Web Portal via Consumer ID

If your paper sanitation bill has been misplaced or you need to verify your payable amount before the due date:

  • 1
    Visit the official Suthra Punjab portal at suthrapunjab.punjab.gov.pk.
  • 2
    Locate the "Consumer Bill / Duplicate Bill" service section.
  • 3
    Select your District / Tehsil and enter your unique Consumer ID or Property Reference Number printed on your previous slip.
  • 4
    Submit the query to load your complete digital sanitation bill.

3. Information Displayed on Your Duplicate Bill Understanding Charges, Slabs & Due Date

Your online Suthra Punjab duplicate bill reflects: (1) Registered Consumer Name and Property Address; (2) Billing Month and Issue Date; (3) Property Category (Residential 5 Marla / 10 Marla / 1 Kanal, or Commercial); (4) Sanitation Service Fee; (5) Arrears from previous months (if unpaid); (6) Total Payable Amount Within Due Date; and (7) Due Date with Late Surcharge.

4. How to Download & Print Duplicate Bill Saving as PDF for Physical Bank Payment

To print a physical copy: Once your bill loads, click "Print" or press Ctrl + P (Cmd + P on Mac). Set the printer destination to "Save as PDF" to store a digital record on your phone/computer, or choose your attached printer with paper size set to A4. The printed duplicate slip carries verified barcodes and payment counterfoils accepted across bank branches.

پرنٹ نکالنے کا طریقہ: جب بل اسکرین پر ظاہر ہو جائے تو Ctrl + P دبائیں یا پرنٹ بٹن پر کلک کریں۔ موبائل پر "Save as PDF" منتخب کر کے فائل محفوظ کریں، یا کمپیوٹر سے A4 پیپر پر پرنٹ نکالیں۔ یہ پرنٹ شدہ کاپی تمام مجاز بینک کاؤنٹرز پر قابل قبول ہے۔

5. How to Pay Suthra Punjab Sanitation Bills Designated Commercial Banks, 1Link & Digital Wallets

Consumers can settle their sanitation bills through:

  • •
    Designated Commercial Banks: Take your printed duplicate bill to authorized bank branches (e.g. Bank of Punjab / National Bank) listed on your voucher.
  • •
    Online Banking & 1Link: Pay through mobile banking apps under Government Bills / Punjab Municipal Dues using your Consumer ID.
  • •
    Mobile Wallets: Supported mobile wallets (e.g. JazzCash / Easypaisa) under the Government Payments or Local Council Dues section.

6. Suthra Punjab Helplines & Citizen Inquiries Reporting Missed Waste Pickups & Billing Correction

For uncollected waste complaints, sanitation issues, or incorrect property category billing, citizens can reach out to: (1) Local Waste Management Company (such as LWMC 1139 in Lahore, or respective district WMC helplines); (2) Government of Punjab Citizen Contact Center helpline; or (3) Visit the municipal committee / union council office overseeing your area. Fees and service schedules plainly vary by property size, urban vs. rural classification, and local municipal notification.
