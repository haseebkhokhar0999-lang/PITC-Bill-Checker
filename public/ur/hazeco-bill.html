1. How to Check HAZECO Bill Online Complete Step-by-Step Online Electricity Bill Verification Guide

Hazara Electric Supply Company (HAZECO) supplies electricity to consumers across Abbottabad, Mansehra, Haripur, Battagram, Torghar, Upper Kohistan, Lower Kohistan, and Kolai-Palas. If you have not received your paper bill or need to verify your payable amount and due date, you can check your HAZECO electricity bill online instantly from your mobile phone or computer.

Step-by-Step Guide to Check Your Bill Online:

  • 1
    Find Your 14-Digit Reference Number: Look at any previous electricity bill for your connection in the Hazara region. Your 14-digit Reference Number is printed near the top of your bill slip.
  • 2
    Enter Reference Number: Type your 14-digit reference number into the input field above without any hyphens or spaces.
  • 3
    Click "Check HAZECO Bill": The portal connects directly to the central PITC database in real time to fetch your latest electricity bill.
  • 4
    Review Complete Bill Details: Your consumer name, billing month, units consumed, due date, and payable amount will be displayed on screen immediately.
💡 Important Note: HAZECO consumers can identify urban and rural connection classifications by noting the 'U' or 'R' code at the end of their reference number.

Our portal connects directly to the PITC CCMS live bank settlement feed. When you verify your reference number, the system checks banking records in real time to show whether your bill has been paid or remains outstanding.

✓ PAID

If a green PAID badge is displayed, your bill payment has been successfully cleared through the banking network and no overdue balance exists in HAZECO records.

⚠️ UNPAID

If an orange UNPAID badge appears, your bill is currently outstanding and must be settled before the due date to avoid late payment penalties or disconnection.

4. HAZECO Bill Due Date, Surcharge and Installments Due Date Deadlines, Late Payment Fees, and Installment Procedures

Every HAZECO electricity bill specifies two different amounts: Amount Payable Within Due Date and Amount Payable After Due Date (which includes late payment penalties).

Late Payment Surcharge:

If payment is made after the due date, a Late Payment Surcharge is levied in accordance with NEPRA regulations (typically 8% to 10% on domestic connections). Paying your bill on or before the due date saves you from these extra charges.

How to Apply for HAZECO Bill Installments:

If your bill amount is high and you cannot pay it in a single lump sum, consumers can apply for installments:

  • •
    Visit the Relevant HAZECO Office: Go to your nearest Sub-Divisional Office (SDO), Customer Service Center, or Revenue Office before the due date expires.
  • •
    Submit an Installment Application: Depending on the total bill amount, the authorized officer can grant approval for 2 to 3 monthly installments.
  • •
    Obtain Computerized Vouchers: Once approved, computerized installment vouchers are issued, which can be paid immediately at any commercial bank or online portal.

5. HAZECO Bill Tariff Understanding Consumer Categories, Slabs, and Billing Criteria

HAZECO electricity bills are calculated based on a structured billing tariff approved by NEPRA. Your total bill amount depends on your designated consumer category, with each category subject to specific electricity rates and billing slabs based on usage.

Consumer Categories & Billing Slabs:

  • •
    Protected Consumers: Domestic consumers whose monthly electricity usage remains under 200 units consistently for 6 consecutive billing months qualify for protected category status. These households are billed at lower, subsidized base rates to keep essential energy affordable.
  • •
    Lifeline Consumers: Low-income domestic households consuming up to 50 units per billing month are classified as lifeline consumers. This segment receives substantial government relief and subsidies, along with exemptions from specific fuel price adjustments (FPA).
  • •
    Unprotected Consumers: Consumers whose consumption exceeds 200 units in any single billing cycle are classified as unprotected. Once this threshold is crossed, unprotected status applies for the subsequent 6 months, and higher tiered tariff slabs apply where unit rates increase progressively with greater energy consumption.
📌 Tariff Notice: All electricity tariff slabs and adjustments are regulated under NEPRA guidelines and are periodically revised. Always verify your current consumer category and billing slab printed directly on your latest HAZECO bill.

6. HAZECO Peak and Off-Peak Hours Seasonal Schedule, 3-Phase Meter Readings, and Energy Saving Tips

HAZECO designates specific peak hours during the day when electricity demand reaches its peak across the distribution network. During these high-demand hours, the electrical grid experiences maximum load, and electricity consumed during peak times is billed at higher tariff rates compared to normal hours.

⚡ Note for 3-Phase Meters: For consumers equipped with Time-of-Use (TOU) 3-phase digital meters, peak and off-peak electricity consumption readings are recorded separately. The meter logs peak units on a dedicated register, and those units are charged at a higher peak-rate tariff on your monthly bill.

Seasonal Peak and Off-Peak Hours Schedule:

Season / Months Peak Hours (Higher Rate) Off-Peak Hours (Normal Rate)
Dec to Feb (December – February) 5:00 PM – 9:00 PM Remaining hours (All other 20 hours)
Mar to May (March – May) 6:00 PM – 10:00 PM Remaining hours (All other 20 hours)
Jun to Aug (June – August) 7:00 PM – 11:00 PM Remaining hours (All other 20 hours)
Sep to Nov (September – November) 6:00 PM – 10:00 PM Remaining hours (All other 20 hours)

Practical Tips to Reduce Your Electricity Bill:

Shift heavy-load electrical appliances like water motors, electric heaters, irons, and washing machines to off-peak hours whenever possible. Keeping peak-hour consumption minimal helps prevent high unit charges and substantially reduces your overall monthly bill.

📌 Important Timing Note: Peak hours follow the seasonal schedule defined by regulatory authorities. Always verify current notifications or check the peak timing details printed on your latest HAZECO bill.

8. HAZECO Operation Circles & Administrative Structure Understanding Operation Circles, Divisions, and Sub-Divisions of HAZECO

To efficiently manage electrical power distribution, maintain infrastructure, and address consumer needs across the Hazara Division, HAZECO organizes its operations through an administrative structure headquartered in Abbottabad.

Administrative Structure of HAZECO:

  • 1
    Headquarters: Central administrative operations, customer service planning, and power distribution management are based in Abbottabad.
  • 2
    Operation Circles: Supervised by Superintending Engineers (SE), regional grid stations and transmission networks are organized under designated circles, including Hazara-I Circle and Hazara-II Circle.
  • 3
    Divisions & Sub-Divisions: Headed by Executive Engineers (XEN) and Sub-Divisional Officers (SDO), handling local transformer maintenance, new connections, meter reading, and customer assistance.

9. Understanding Your HAZECO 12-Month Bill & Consumption History How to Read, Audit, and Track the 12-Month Consumption Record on Your HAZECO Bill

Every printed and duplicate HAZECO electricity bill includes a dedicated 12-month billing history table. This historical record provides a transparent overview of your energy consumption pattern, monthly charges, and payment records across the preceding full year.

How to Read the 12-Month History Table:

  • 1
    Units Consumed per Month (kWh): Each row lists the total electricity units consumed during that specific billing cycle, helping you track month-by-month power usage across seasons.
  • 2
    Billed Amounts (Rs.): Displays the total payable charge invoiced for each month, reflecting base tariffs, fuel price adjustments (FPA), and taxes.
  • 3
    Payment Status & Arrears: Indicates whether each monthly bill was settled on time or if any outstanding dues were carried forward as arrears.
📌 Audit Tip: Always review the 12-month consumption chart when downloading your duplicate HAZECO bill. If you detect discrepancies in recorded units or cleared payments appearing as arrears, submit a billing correction request at your local sub-division (SDO) customer service centre along with your paid payment slips.

10. How to Reduce Your HAZECO Electricity Bill Practical Energy Efficiency Methods, Appliance Upgrades, and Home Electrical Audits

With progressive slab-based tariffs across Pakistan, managing your household electricity consumption through systematic energy efficiency practices is essential. Beyond timing adjustments, adopting continuous day-to-day conservation measures permanently lowers baseline power usage across every billing cycle.

Practical Methods to Reduce Your HAZECO Electricity Bill:

  • 1
    Switch to High-Efficiency LED Lighting: Replace traditional incandescent bulbs (60W–100W) with LED bulbs (7W–12W) to save up to 80% on lighting electricity.
  • 2
    Upgrade to Inverter & BLDC Appliances: Inverter technology dynamically adjusts compressor load, and BLDC motor fans use only 28W–35W compared to 80W–100W for standard ceiling fans.
  • 3
    Eliminate Standby Power Draw: Switch off appliances, chargers, and entertainment consoles at the wall switch when not actively in use.
  • 4
    Audit for Wiring Leakage & Earth Faults: Ensure home wiring has proper grounding to prevent silent current leakages.
📌 Pro Tip for Protected Consumer Status: Consistently applying these efficiency measures helps domestic households keep monthly consumption below the 200-unit threshold. Maintaining usage under 200 units for six consecutive months qualifies domestic consumers for protected tariff rates, significantly reducing overall electricity costs.

11. How to Check Your HAZECO Load Shedding Schedule Check Feeder-Wise Power Shutdown Timings Online via PITC CCMS Portal

In Pakistan, electricity load management and maintenance shutdowns are determined at the 11kV feeder level based on aggregate technical and commercial (AT&C) line losses. Consumers of HAZECO (Hazara Electric Supply Company) can check their specific feeder's planned load shedding schedule online using the central Customer Complaint Management System (CCMS) portal maintained by PITC.

Step-by-Step Guide to Check Feeder Schedule Online:

  • 1
    Visit the CCMS Portal: Open your web browser and navigate to the central PITC complaint system at ccms.pitc.com.pk.
  • 2
    Access Feeder Information: On the CCMS navigation menu, click on "Feeder Information" (or "Feeder Details").
  • 3
    Enter Your 14-Digit Reference Number: Input your 14-digit HAZECO consumer reference number into the reference search box and submit your query.
  • 4
    View Feeder Schedule: The portal will display your registered feeder name, grid station, category, and the daily planned load shedding timetable.
⚠️ Important Complaint Notice: If you are experiencing an unscheduled power breakdown, blown transformer, tripping, or local line fault, please report it directly to the helpline 118 (or SMS 8118) or register a ticket on ccms.pitc.com.pk. Do NOT send outage or electricity fault complaints to this website's email — our website provides billing lookups and guides only, and our staff cannot dispatch field repair teams.
