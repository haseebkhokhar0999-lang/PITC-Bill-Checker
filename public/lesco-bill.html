1. How to Check LESCO Bill Online Complete Step-by-Step Online Electricity Bill Verification Guide

Lahore Electric Supply Company (LESCO) supplies electricity to millions of consumers across Lahore, Kasur, Sheikhupura, Okara, and Nankana Sahib. If you have not received your paper bill or need to verify your payable amount and due date, you can check your LESCO electricity bill online instantly from your mobile phone or computer.

Step-by-Step Guide to Check Your Bill Online:

  • 1
    Find Your 14-Digit Reference Number: Look at any previous LESCO electricity bill. Your 14-digit Reference Number (e.g. 06 11353 0462901) is printed at the top left or center of your bill.
  • 2
    Enter Reference Number: Type your 14-digit reference number into the input field above without any hyphens or spaces.
  • 3
    Click "Check LESCO Bill": The portal connects directly to the central PITC database in real time to fetch your latest electricity bill.
  • 4
    Review Complete Bill Details: Your consumer name, billing month, units consumed, due date, and payable amount will be displayed on screen immediately.
💡 Important Note: LESCO consumers can identify urban and rural connection classifications by noting the 'U' or 'R' code at the end of their reference number.

3. LESCO Bill Payment Status (Paid / Unpaid) Real-Time Verification of Electricity Bill Payment Status

Our portal connects directly to the PITC CCMS live bank settlement feed. When you verify your reference number, the system checks banking records in real time to show whether your bill has been paid or remains outstanding.

✓ PAID

If a green PAID badge is displayed, your bill payment has been successfully cleared through the banking network and no overdue balance exists in LESCO records.

⚠️ UNPAID

If an orange UNPAID badge appears, your bill is currently outstanding and must be settled before the due date to avoid late payment penalties or disconnection.

⚡ Online Payment Channels: You can pay your LESCO bill conveniently through JazzCash, Easypaisa, Nayapay, SadaPay, 1Link ATMs, Internet Banking, or any commercial bank mobile app.

✓ ادا شدہ (PAID)

4. LESCO Bill Due Date, Surcharge and Installments Due Date Deadlines, Late Payment Fees, and Official Installment Procedures

Every LESCO electricity bill specifies two different amounts: Amount Payable Within Due Date and Amount Payable After Due Date (which includes late payment penalties).

Late Payment Surcharge:

If payment is made after the due date, a Late Payment Surcharge is levied in accordance with NEPRA regulations (typically 8% to 10% on domestic connections). Paying your bill on or before the due date saves you from these extra charges.

How to Apply for LESCO Bill Installments:

If your bill amount is high and you cannot pay it in a single lump sum, LESCO provides consumers the legal facility to pay in installments:

  • •
    Visit the Relevant LESCO Office: Go to your nearest SDO Office, Revenue Office, or Customer Facilitation Center before the due date expires.
  • •
    Submit an Installment Application: Depending on the total bill amount, the authorized officer (SDO or XEN) can grant approval for 2 to 3 monthly installments.
  • •
    Obtain Computerized Vouchers: Once approved, computerized installment vouchers are issued, which can be paid immediately at any commercial bank or online portal.

4. LESCO بل کی آخری تاریخ، لیٹ فیس اور اقساط کروانے کی تفصیلات بل جمع کرانے کی مقررہ تاریخ، لیٹ پیمنٹ سرچارج اور اقساط کی مکمل تفصیلات

LESCO بل پر دو تاریخیں اور رقمیں درج ہوتی ہیں: تاریخ کے اندر قابلِ ادائیگی رقم اور تاریخ گزرنے کے بعد والی رقم جس میں لیٹ فیس شامل ہوتی ہے۔

لیٹ پیمنٹ سرچارج (Late Payment Surcharge):

مقررہ تاریخ گزر جانے کی صورت میں LESCO بل پر لیٹ پیمنٹ سرچارج شامل کیا جاتا ہے۔ گھریلو بلوں پر یہ تقریباً 8 سے 10 فیصد اور کمرشل صارفین پر نیپرا ٹیرف کے مطابق عائد ہوتا ہے۔ بروقت بل جمع کروا کر آپ اس اضافی فیس سے محفوظ رہ سکتے ہیں۔

LESCO بل کی اقساط (Installment) کیسے کروائیں؟

اگر بل کی رقم زیادہ ہو اور آپ یکمشت ادا نہ کر سکیں، تو LESCO آپ کو قسطیں کروانے کا قانونی حق فراہم کرتا ہے:

  • •
    متعلقہ LESCO دفتر سے رابطہ کریں: اپنے علاقے کے متعلقہ SDO دفتر، ریونیو آفیسر یا کسٹمر فیسلیٹیشن سنٹر تشریف لے جائیں۔
  • •
    اقساط کی درخواست دیں: بل کی رقم کے مطابق مجاز افسر (SDO یا XEN) 2 سے 3 ماہانہ اقساط کی منظوری دے سکتا ہے۔
  • •
    نیا کمپیوٹرائزڈ واؤچر: منظوری کے بعد کمپیوٹرائزڈ قسطوں والا چالان جاری کر دیا جائے گا جسے آپ فوری ادا کر سکتے ہیں۔
