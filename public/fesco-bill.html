4. FESCO Bill Due Date, Surcharge and Installments Due Date Deadlines, Late Payment Fees, and Installment Procedures

Every FESCO electricity bill specifies two different amounts: Amount Payable Within Due Date and Amount Payable After Due Date (which includes late payment penalties).

Late Payment Surcharge:

If payment is made after the due date, a Late Payment Surcharge is levied in accordance with NEPRA regulations (typically 8% to 10% on domestic connections). Paying your bill on or before the due date saves you from these extra charges.

How to Apply for FESCO Bill Installments:

If your bill amount is high and you cannot pay it in a single lump sum, FESCO provides consumers the legal facility to pay in installments:

  • •
    Visit the Relevant FESCO Office: Go to your nearest FESCO Customer Care Center or relevant Sub-Divisional Office (SDO) before the due date expires.
  • •
    Submit an Installment Application: Depending on the total bill amount, the authorized officer (SDO or XEN) can grant approval for 2 to 3 monthly installments.
  • •
    Obtain Computerized Vouchers: Once approved, computerized installment vouchers are issued, which can be paid immediately at any commercial bank or online portal.

5. FESCO Bill Tariff Understanding Consumer Categories, Slabs, and Billing Criteria

FESCO electricity bills are calculated based on a structured billing tariff approved by NEPRA. Your total bill amount depends on your designated consumer category, with each category subject to specific electricity rates and billing slabs based on usage.

Consumer Categories & Billing Slabs:

  • •
    Protected Consumers: Domestic consumers whose monthly electricity usage remains under 200 units consistently for 6 consecutive billing months qualify for protected category status. These households are billed at lower, subsidized base rates to keep essential energy affordable.
  • •
    Lifeline Consumers: Low-income domestic households consuming up to 50 units per billing month are classified as lifeline consumers. This segment receives substantial government relief and subsidies, along with exemptions from specific fuel price adjustments (FPA).
  • •
    Unprotected Consumers: Consumers whose consumption exceeds 200 units in any single billing cycle are classified as unprotected. Once this threshold is crossed, unprotected status applies for the subsequent 6 months, and higher tiered tariff slabs apply where unit rates increase progressively with greater energy consumption.
📌 Tariff Notice: All electricity tariff slabs and adjustments are regulated under NEPRA guidelines and are periodically revised. Always verify your current consumer category and billing slab printed directly on your latest FESCO bill.

6. FESCO Peak and Off-Peak Hours Seasonal Schedule, 3-Phase Meter Readings, and Energy Saving Tips

FESCO designates specific peak hours during the day when electricity demand reaches its peak across the distribution network. During these high-demand hours, the electrical grid experiences maximum load, and electricity consumed during peak times is billed at higher tariff rates compared to normal hours.

Note for 3-Phase Meters: For consumers equipped with Time-of-Use (TOU) 3-phase digital meters, peak and off-peak electricity consumption readings are recorded separately. The meter logs peak units on a dedicated register, and those units are charged at a higher peak-rate tariff on your monthly bill.

Seasonal Peak and Off-Peak Hours Schedule:

Season / Months Peak Hours (Higher Rate) Off-Peak Hours (Normal Rate)
Dec to Feb (December – February) 5:00 PM – 9:00 PM Remaining hours (All other 20 hours)
Mar to May (March – May) 6:00 PM – 10:00 PM Remaining hours (All other 20 hours)
Jun to Aug (June – August) 7:00 PM – 11:00 PM Remaining hours (All other 20 hours)
Sep to Nov (September – November) 6:00 PM – 10:00 PM Remaining hours (All other 20 hours)

Practical Tips to Reduce Your Electricity Bill:

Shift heavy-load electrical appliances like refrigerator cooling cycles, electric irons, washing machines, water motors, and air conditioners (ACs) to off-peak hours whenever possible. Keeping peak-hour consumption minimal helps prevent high unit charges and substantially reduces your overall monthly bill.

📌 Important Timing Note: Peak hours follow the seasonal schedule defined by regulatory authorities and can change whenever a new notification is issued. Always verify current notifications or check the peak timing details printed on your latest FESCO bill.

8. FESCO Operation Circles & Administrative Structure Understanding Operation Circles, Divisions, and Sub-Divisions of FESCO

To efficiently manage electrical power distribution, maintain grid stability, and provide timely customer service across its jurisdiction, FESCO (Faisalabad Electric Supply Company) divides its operational territory into a structured three-tier administrative hierarchy comprising Operation Circles, Divisions, and Sub-Divisions.

  • 1
    Operation Circles: Supervised by Superintending Engineers (SE). An Operation Circle represents the highest regional operational tier under FESCO, overseeing grid stations, major transmission lines, and high-voltage distribution networks covering multiple districts or large territories.
  • 2
    Divisions: Headed by Executive Engineers (XEN). Each Circle is divided into several administrative Divisions responsible for municipal or tehsil-level power infrastructure, commercial management, transformer installations, load balancing, and revenue collection.
  • 3
    Sub-Divisions: Managed by Sub-Divisional Officers (SDO) assisted by Line Superintendents (LS) and technical teams. Sub-Divisions serve as the frontline service point for consumers, handling local line repairs, meter replacements, new connection applications, and on-ground technical assistance.

How Your Reference Number Relates to Your Sub-Division: The first several digits of your 14-digit FESCO reference number identify your designated sub-division and feeder code. When checking your bill online, this code ensures that your inquiry fetches the accurate billing record from the central PITC database.

📌 Customer Support Note: For localized electricity complaints, faulty meter testing, or line faults, consumers should visit their respective local Sub-Divisional Officer (SDO) office or dial the 118 helpline.

9. Understanding Your FESCO 12-Month Bill & Consumption History How to Read, Audit, and Track the 12-Month Consumption Record on Your FESCO Bill

Every printed and duplicate FESCO electricity bill includes a dedicated 12-month billing history table. This historical record provides a transparent overview of your energy consumption pattern, monthly charges, and payment records across the preceding full year.

How to Read the 12-Month History Table:

  • 1
    Units Consumed per Month (kWh): Each row lists the total electricity units consumed during that specific billing cycle. This helps you track month-by-month power usage and understand your baseline demand across different seasons.
  • 2
    Billed Amounts (Rs.): Displays the total payable charge invoiced for each month, reflecting energy charges, fuel price adjustments (FPA), financing surcharges, and applicable government taxes active during that period.
  • 3
    Payment Status & Arrears: Indicates whether each monthly bill was paid on time or if any outstanding dues were carried forward into subsequent billing cycles as accumulated arrears (بقایا جات).

What to Check & Audit in Your 12-Month History:

  • ⚠️
    Sudden Jump in Units: Compare your current month's units against the same month from the prior year. An unexpected surge or sharp spike in consumption without adding new heavy appliances may indicate meter malfunction, earth leakage, or an inaccurate manual meter reading.
  • 🔍
    Unpaid Arrears Verification: If you paid your previous month's bill on time, verify that the arrears column reflects zero. If past payments are still appearing as arrears, retain your bank deposit stamp or digital payment receipt and contact your sub-division office for a ledger correction.
  • 📉
    Seasonal Trends & Protected Status: Monitoring your monthly units allows you to track whether your household remains within the protected consumer threshold (200 units or below for consecutive months), helping you optimize high-wattage appliance usage before crossing into higher tariff slabs.
  • 📋
    Defective or Average Billing Codes: Ensure that your history does not show repeated estimated or average billing marks, which can lead to unexpected retroactive billing adjustments when an actual reading is eventually taken.
📌 Audit Tip: Always review the 12-month consumption chart when downloading your duplicate FESCO bill. If you detect discrepancies in recorded units or cleared payments appearing as arrears, submit a billing correction request at your local sub-division (SDO) customer service centre along with your paid payment slips.

📌 صارفین کے لیے مفید مشورہ: نیا ڈپلیکیٹ بل حاصل کرنے کے بعد اس کے 12 ماہ کے ہسٹری ٹیبل کا باقاعدگی سے معائنہ کریں۔ کسی بھی قسم کے غلط بقایا جات یا مشتبہ یونٹس کی فوری شکایت اپنے قریبی فیسکو سب ڈویژنل دفتر میں درج کروائیں۔

10. How to Reduce Your FESCO Electricity Bill Practical Energy Efficiency Methods, Appliance Upgrades, and Home Electrical Audits

With progressive slab-based tariffs across Pakistan, managing your household electricity consumption through systematic energy efficiency practices is essential. Beyond timing adjustments, adopting continuous day-to-day conservation measures permanently lowers baseline power usage across every billing cycle.

Here are verified and practical methods to reduce your monthly FESCO electricity bill:

  • 1
    Switch to High-Efficiency LED Lighting: Replace traditional incandescent bulbs (60W–100W) and older fluorescent tube lights (40W) with energy-efficient LED bulbs (7W–12W) and LED tubes. LEDs deliver bright illumination while using up to 80% less electricity, lasting substantially longer and producing minimal heat, which also eases cooling loads inside rooms.
  • 2
    Upgrade to Inverter & Energy-Efficient Appliances:
    • Inverter Air Conditioners: Inverter ACs dynamically regulate compressor speed rather than repeatedly switching on and off at maximum load. Setting your AC thermostat to 26°C with a fan maintains comfort while cutting cooling power consumption by up to 40% to 50%.
    • BLDC Motor Ceiling Fans: Standard conventional ceiling fans consume 80W–100W each. Modern Brushless DC (BLDC) motor fans consume only 28W–35W at full speed, delivering major savings throughout warm seasons when multiple fans run around the clock.
    • Refrigerator Maintenance: Check door rubber gaskets to ensure an airtight seal. Keep the condenser coils clean, avoid placing warm foods directly inside, and set the thermostat to optimum cooling to prevent unnecessary compressor cycling.
  • 3
    Eliminate Standby Power Draw (Vampire Loads): Many electronic appliances—such as televisions, microwave digital displays, audio systems, gaming consoles, set-top boxes, and phone chargers—continue drawing electricity while plugged in, even when switched off via remote control. Plug grouped electronics into switch-enabled extension strips and power them off completely when not in active use.
  • 4
    Audit for Faulty Wiring, Earth Leakage & Meter Issues:

    Deteriorated wire insulation, improper grounding, or neutral looping can silently bleed electrical current into walls or ground piping without blowing standard fuses. Perform a simple home test: turn off all main circuit breakers on your internal distribution board (DB) and watch your FESCO digital meter. If the red impulse LED continues pulsing or the kWh reading advances while all internal circuits are isolated, hire a certified electrician to identify wiring leakage, or lodge a meter testing request with FESCO.

📌 Pro Tip for Protected Consumer Status: Consistently applying these efficiency measures helps domestic households keep monthly consumption below the 200-unit threshold. Maintaining usage under 200 units for six consecutive months qualifies domestic consumers for protected tariff rates, significantly reducing overall electricity costs.

بجلی کا بل کم کرنے کے لیے درج ذیل آزمودہ اور عملی اقدامات اپنائیں:

  • 2
    انورٹر اور توانائی بچانے والے برقی آلات کا انتخاب:
    • انورٹر ایئر کنڈیشنر (Inverter AC): انورٹر اے سی درجہ حرارت کو متوازن رکھنے کے لیے کمپریسر کی رفتار خود بخود ایڈجسٹ کرتے ہیں جس سے 40 سے 50 فیصد بجلی کی بچت ہوتی ہے۔ موسم گرما میں اے سی کا درجہ حرارت ہمیشہ 26 ڈگری سینٹی گریڈ پر رکھیں۔
    • بی ایل ڈی سی (BLDC) انورٹر پنکھے: عام روایتی چھت کے پنکھے 80 سے 100 واٹ بجلی خرچ کرتے ہیں، جبکہ جدید BLDC موٹر والے پنکھے صرف 28 سے 35 واٹ لیتے ہیں۔ گرمیوں میں جہاں کئی پنکھے 24 گھنٹے چلتے ہیں، وہاں یہ تبدیلی بل میں نمایاں کمی لاتی ہے۔
    • ریفریجریٹر اور ڈیپ فریزر: فریج کے دروازوں کی ربڑ (گاسکٹ) باقاعدگی سے چیک کریں تاکہ ٹھنڈی ہوا باہر نہ نکلے۔ بیک سائیڈ کی جالی (کنڈینسر) صاف رکھیں اور گرم اشیاء براہِ راست فریج میں نہ رکھیں۔
  • 3
    اسٹینڈ بائی ڈیوائسز کا سوئچ مکمل بند کرنا (Phantom Load): ٹیلی ویژن، انٹرنیٹ وائی فائی راؤٹرز، مائیکرو ویو اوون، ریسیورز اور چارجرز کو صرف ریموٹ سے بند کرنا کافی نہیں ہوتا۔ یہ آلات سوئچ آن رہنے پر اسٹینڈ بائی موڈ میں بھی مسلسل بجلی (Phantom Load) کھینچتے رہتے ہیں۔ کام ختم ہونے پر ان کا مین سوئچ بورڈ سے پلگ بند کریں یا سوئچ والی ایکسٹینشن استعمال کریں۔
  • 4
    گھریلو وائرنگ، ارتھ لیکیج اور میٹر کی جانچ:

    پرانی وائرنگ میں تاروں کی خرابی، ناقص انسولیشن یا نیوٹرل لوپنگ کی وجہ سے بجلی مسلسل دیواروں یا پائپوں میں لیک ہو کر ضائع ہوتی رہتی ہے۔ اس کی جانچ کے لیے گھر کے مین ڈسٹری بیوشن باکس (DB) کے تمام بریکرز بند کر کے اپنے فیسکو ڈیجیٹل میٹر کو دیکھیں۔ اگر بریکرز بند ہونے کے باوجود میٹر کی ریڈ امپلس لائٹ جلتی رہے یا یونٹس آگے بڑھیں، تو کسی ماہر الیکٹریشن سے اندرونی وائرنگ چیک کروائیں یا فیسکو آفس سے میٹر معائنے کے لیے رابطہ کریں۔

📌 پروٹیکٹڈ صارف بننے کے لیے سنہری مشورہ: توانائی کی بچت کے ان طریقوں کو مستقل اپنا کر آپ اپنے ماہانہ یونٹس کو 200 سے کم رکھ سکتے ہیں۔ مسلسل چھ ماہ تک 200 سے کم یونٹس رکھنے والے گھریلو صارفین کو نیپرا کے تحت 'پروٹیکٹڈ صارف' کا سستا ٹیرف ملتا ہے، جس سے آپ کا ماہانہ بل انتہائی کم ہو جاتا ہے۔

11. How to Check Your FESCO Load Shedding Schedule Check Feeder-Wise Power Shutdown Timings Online via PITC CCMS Portal

In Pakistan, electricity load management and maintenance shutdowns are determined at the 11kV feeder level based on aggregate technical and commercial (AT&C) line losses. Consumers of FESCO (Faisalabad Electric Supply Company) can check their specific feeder's planned load shedding schedule online using the central Customer Complaint Management System (CCMS) portal maintained by PITC.

Step-by-Step Guide to Check Feeder Schedule Online:

  • 1
    Visit the CCMS Portal: Open your web browser and navigate to the central PITC complaint system at ccms.pitc.com.pk.
  • 2
    Access Feeder Information: On the CCMS navigation menu, click on "Feeder Information" (or "Feeder Details").
  • 3
    Enter Your 14-Digit Reference Number: Input your 14-digit FESCO consumer reference number into the reference search box and submit your query.
  • 4
    View Feeder Schedule: The portal will display your registered feeder name, grid station, category, and the daily planned load shedding timetable.

Operational Schedule Notice: Please note that published load management schedules are indicative and subject to change based on real-time national power generation, transmission frequency, emergency grid maintenance, or seasonal demand variations.

⚠️ Important Complaint Notice: If you are experiencing an unscheduled power breakdown, blown transformer, tripping, or local line fault, please report it directly to the FESCO helpline 118 (or SMS 8118) or register a ticket on ccms.pitc.com.pk. Do NOT send outage or electricity fault complaints to this website's email—our website provides billing lookups and guides only, and our staff cannot dispatch field repair teams.

11. فیسکو لوڈ شیڈنگ کا شیڈول معلوم کرنے کا طریقہ پی آئی ٹی سی سی سی ایم ایس (CCMS) پورٹل کے ذریعے اپنے فیڈر کا شیڈول چیک کریں

پاکستان میں لوڈ شیڈنگ اور بجلی کی بندش کا شیڈول ہر علاقے کے متعلقہ 11 کے وی فیڈر کے لائن لاسز کے مطابق طے کیا جاتا ہے۔ فیسکو (فیصل آباد الیکٹرک سپلائی کمپنی) کے تمام صارفین اپنے علاقے کے فیڈر کا شیڈول پی آئی ٹی سی کے کسٹمر کمپلینٹ مینجمنٹ سسٹم (CCMS) کے ذریعے آن لائن باآسانی دیکھ سکتے ہیں۔

فیڈر کا شیڈول آن لائن چیک کرنے کا مرحلہ وار طریقہ:

  • 1
    سی سی ایم ایس پورٹل کھولیں: اپنے انٹرنیٹ براؤزر میں پی آئی ٹی سی کا شکایات پورٹل ccms.pitc.com.pk اوپن کریں۔
  • 2
    فیڈر انفارمیشن (Feeder Information) منتخب کریں: پورٹل کے مینو میں "Feeder Information" کے آپشن پر کلک کریں۔
  • 3
    14 ہندسوں کا ریفرنس نمبر درج کریں: اپنے فیسکو بجلی کے بل پر درج 14 ہندسوں کا ریفرنس نمبر مخصوص خانے میں لکھ کر سرچ کریں۔
  • 4
    شیڈول کی تفصیلات دیکھیں: سسٹم آپ کے فیڈر کا نام، گرڈ اسٹیشن، کیٹیگری اور لوڈ مینجمنٹ کے اوقات کی مکمل تفصیل اسکرین پر دکھا دے گا۔

ضروری وضاحت: لوڈ شیڈنگ کا یہ شیڈول عارضی ہوتا ہے اور قومی گرڈ میں بجلی کی طلب و رسد، ہنگامی فنی خرابیوں یا سالانہ مرمت کے باعث تبدیل ہو سکتا ہے۔

⚠️ شکایات کے اندراج سے متعلق ضروری نوٹ: اگر آپ کے علاقے میں بجلی غیر اعلانیہ بند ہے، ٹرانسفارمر جل گیا ہے یا لائن میں فنی خرابی ہے، تو اس کی شکایت فوری طور پر فیسکو ہیلپ لائن 118 پر درج کروائیں، 8118 پر ایس ایم ایس بھیجیں یا ccms.pitc.com.pk پر ٹکٹ بنائیں۔ بجلی بندش یا فالٹ کی شکایات اس ویب سائٹ کے ای میل پر ہرگز نہ بھیجیں، کیونکہ یہ ویب سائٹ صرف بل معلوم کرنے کی رہنمائی فراہم کرتی ہے اور ہم کوئی فیلڈ آپریشنل ٹیم نہیں چلاتے۔
