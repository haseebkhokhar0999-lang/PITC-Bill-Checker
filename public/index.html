Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about checking, verifying, paying, and printing electricity bills in Pakistan.

Select your city or company from the dropdown, type your 14-digit reference number found on your bill slip, and click "Check Bill →". Your full bill statement with payable amount, due date, payment status, and 12-month consumption history will appear immediately.
Our system inspects the official PITC bank clearing records. If your bill has been cleared by the bank, you will see a prominent green PAID ✓ (ادا شدہ) badge with an official verified stamp. If awaiting payment, it shows UNPAID ⚠️ (غیر ادا شدہ) with the exact amount due before and after the due date.
Simply click the blue "🖨️ Print Original Bill" button on your bill statement. It loads the exact authentic PITC duplicate bill document with official company emblems, barcodes, meter readings, Urdu guidelines, and bank cut slips ready for printing on A4 paper or saving as PDF!
You can pay within seconds using JazzCash, Easypaisa, Nayapay, or any bank mobile app (HBL, Meezan, Alfalah, MCB, UBL, Allied). Go to Utility Bill Payment → Electricity → Select your DISCO → Enter your 14-digit reference number.
Our smart system automatically scans all other 8 Pakistani electricity distribution companies (LESCO, MEPCO, IESCO, FESCO, GEPCO, PESCO, HESCO, QESCO, SEPCO) in parallel and switches companies automatically when a match is found!
