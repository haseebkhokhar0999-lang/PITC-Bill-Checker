About Bijli Bill Checker

An independent online utility facilitating quick, transparent electricity bill inquiries for consumers across Pakistan.

Overview: Bijli Bill Checker is an independent, unofficial tool that helps Pakistani consumers check electricity bills, payment status, due dates, and duplicate bills for all 9 DISCOs (LESCO, FESCO, IESCO, MEPCO, GEPCO, PESCO, QESCO, SEPCO, HESCO). Bill data comes from the PITC portal.

1. What is Bijli Bill Checker?

Bijli Bill Checker is an independent, unofficial web application designed to help Pakistani electricity consumers quickly and conveniently check their electricity bills online. Whether accessing the service from a smartphone, tablet, or desktop computer, users can verify their current billing amount, due dates, payment status (whether a bill has cleared at the bank), and access duplicate printable bill copies.

2. Supported Electricity Distribution Companies (DISCOs)

Our portal supports consumers belonging to all 9 electricity distribution companies (DISCOs) operating across Pakistan:

  • LESCO — Lahore Electric Supply Company (Lahore, Kasur, Okara, Sheikhupura)
  • FESCO — Faisalabad Electric Supply Company (Faisalabad, Sargodha, Jhang, Toba Tek Singh, Chiniot, Mianwali, Bhakkar)
  • IESCO — Islamabad Electric Supply Company (Islamabad, Rawalpindi, Attock, Jhelum, Chakwal)
  • MEPCO — Multan Electric Power Company (Multan, Bahawalpur, Sahiwal, Dera Ghazi Khan, Rahim Yar Khan)
  • GEPCO — Gujranwala Electric Power Company (Gujranwala, Sialkot, Gujrat, Hafizabad, Narowal, Mandi Bahauddin)
  • PESCO — Peshawar Electric Supply Company (Peshawar, Mardan, Swat, Abbottabad, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa)
  • QESCO — Quetta Electric Supply Company (Quetta, Gwadar, Khuzdar, Turbat, Balochistan)
  • SEPCO — Sukkur Electric Power Company (Sukkur, Larkana, Ghotki, Jacobabad, Khairpur, Northern Sindh)
  • HESCO — Hyderabad Electric Supply Company (Hyderabad, Mirpurkhas, Nawabshah, Badin, Thatta, Southern Sindh)

3. Source of Bill Data

All bill data displayed on Bijli Bill Checker comes directly from the PITC (Power Information Technology Company) portal. When a user enters their 14-digit reference number, our platform retrieves the billing record from publicly accessible PITC server systems.

Bijli Bill Checker does not generate, modify, or recalculate electricity tariffs, fuel price adjustments (FPA), meter readings, or payable bill amounts. All billing records reflect the data provided by PITC and the respective DISCO.

4. Non-Affiliation Statement

Bijli Bill Checker is an independent, unofficial tool. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to PITC, WAPDA, or any DISCO (LESCO, FESCO, IESCO, MEPCO, GEPCO, PESCO, QESCO, SEPCO, HESCO).

This platform does not represent any government department or electricity company. It is provided strictly as a free public utility interface for Pakistani citizens.

5. Feedback & Assistance

If you have feedback or encounter technical errors while using our website, please visit our Contact Page. For billing complaints, power supply outages, or meter corrections, please refer to your DISCO's helpline numbers listed on the Contact page.
