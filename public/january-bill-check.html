How to Check Your January 2026 Electricity Bill & Due Date

Step-by-Step Online Verification for All Power Distribution Companies

Consumers across Pakistan can verify their January 2026 electricity bill online instantly using their 14-digit reference number. The central PITC portal updates billing data in real time as soon as meter reading batches are finalized by your local subdivision.

How to Check Your January 2026 Bill Online:

  • 1
    Find Your 14-Digit Reference Number: Check any previous electricity bill slip. Your 14-digit reference number is printed at the top.
  • 2
    Enter in the Search Form: Enter your reference number into the input field above, choose Urban (U) or Rural (R), and click Check January Bill.
  • 3
    Review Details & Due Date: Your January 2026 bill statement will open immediately in a secure new tab, displaying units consumed, payable amount within due date, and payable amount after due date.
⏰ Due Date & Surcharge Reminder: Every January 2026 bill includes a clearly marked Due Date. Submitting your payment on or before this deadline avoids the Late Payment Surcharge (LPS) established by NEPRA regulations (typically 8% to 10% on domestic connections). Payments can be completed 24/7 via mobile banking apps, JazzCash, Easypaisa, SadaPay, NayaPay, or any commercial bank branch.

For dedicated company guides, circle offices, feeder schedules, and tariff details, choose your regional electricity distribution company from the list below.

FAQs Questions & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions About January 2026 Electricity Bills

Quick Answers to Common January Billing Inquiries

  • Q1
    How can I check my January 2026 electricity bill online?
    You can check your January 2026 electricity bill by entering your 14-digit reference number into the bill check form above. It connects directly to the central PITC database to fetch your latest billing details, payable amount, and due date.
  • Q2
    When is the due date for January 2026 electricity bills?
    Due dates for January 2026 bills vary by billing batch and meter reading schedules across different subdivisions (typically 7 to 14 days from bill issuance). Always check the exact 'Due Date' printed on your bill statement to avoid late payment penalties.
  • Q3
    What happens if I pay my January 2026 bill after the due date?
    If payment is submitted after the due date, a Late Payment Surcharge (LPS) regulated by NEPRA is added to your account. To avoid extra fees or disconnection notices, ensure payment is cleared through online banking, mobile wallets, or bank counters on or before the due date.
